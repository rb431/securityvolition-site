Most CTF calendars are built for red teamers. If you work the defensive side, finding forensics and blue-team practice usually means digging through general listings to see which ones include forensics challenges.

So I built one place for it: the Security Volition CTF Board, https://ctf.securityvolition.com

It tracks upcoming DFIR, forensics, and blue-team CTFs with confirmed dates. The next few weeks include WolvCTF, SANS DFIR NetWars in Paris and Amsterdam, and the Guardians blue-team qualifier in February. Events whose dates aren't confirmed yet, like the SANS Holiday Hack Challenge and the Magnet Virtual Summit CTF, sit in a separate section. It also lists always-on labs like CyberDefenders, Blue Team Labs Online, and Hack The Box Sherlocks for when there's no event on the calendar.

The defensive angle: what IR teams are seeing is that the gap usually isn't tooling, it's reps. Analysts who regularly work timelines, memory, and log evidence under a clock move faster when it's real. A few hours of forensics CTF a month is cheap practice for that.

If I'm missing an event, tell me and I'll add it.
